DF · 圆盘翻转Disk Flip

Disk Flip 考察在公开的速度与接触力限制下轻柔翻转桌面盘体：取得可抓持的悬伸后夹起翻面，放回目标区域，完全撤手并稳定保持 2 秒。原版 DF-01 保留固定的推边、双手夹持流程与原评分器；扩展设置改变盘体（实心圆盘、带沿浅盘、受力敏感浅盘）和身体（G1＋Dex3、G1＋Inspire 五指手、Stretch 3＋夹爪），并接受重抓、桌面支撑与受控交接等任何合法策略。Disk Flip tests gently flipping a tabletop plate under public speed and contact-force limits: obtain a graspable overhang, pick it up and turn it over, place it in the goal area, release completely and hold still for 2 s. The original DF-01 keeps its fixed push-to-edge and bimanual-clamp sequence and original scorer; the extension settings vary the plate (solid disk, rimmed plate, load-sensitive plate) and the body (G1 with Dex3, G1 with Inspire five-finger hands, Stretch 3 with a gripper), accepting any legal strategy including regrasps, table support and controlled hand-overs.
