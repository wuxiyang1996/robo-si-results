H2 · 推车辅助重物运输Cart-assisted heavy transport

G1 抬不起、也无法在地面推动 20 kg 货箱，要借滚轮导轨把货箱推上托盘车，再分段换侧推车穿过两道错位通道，让货箱留在车上交付到目标区。扩展在同一世界里换身体或改路线，考察机器人能否依据自身能力判断可行路线：通向死巷的近路（B01）、推力够大可不用车的 R1 及 Y/N 交付规则（B03）、低位短行程的 Stretch 3 分段推送（B04）。另有一项不可完成对照（I02）：通道收窄后载货组合体过不去，正确行为是空手进入 Q 区停稳。The G1 can neither lift nor floor-push the 20 kg crate, so it must push it along roller rails onto a pallet cart and then push the cart, changing sides, through two offset passages so the crate is delivered on the cart in the goal zone. The extension changes the body or the route in the same world to test whether the robot judges feasible routes from its own abilities: a shortcut into a dead end (B01), an R1 strong enough to skip the cart, with Y/N delivery rules (B03), and a low, short-stroke Stretch 3 that must push in segments (B04). One infeasible control (I02) narrows the passages so the loaded cart cannot pass; the correct behaviour is to exit empty-handed into zone Q and stand still.
