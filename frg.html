FRG · 墙角冰箱移出Corner appliance extraction

自由基座 Unitree G1（Dex3 手）在 Genesis GPU 接触物理中，把卡在墙角的紧凑冰箱整体移入地面目标区。冰箱背面和两侧受墙或固定柜子限制，原地转不开，只能经正面横把手拉出、沿地面拖移后松手静置。考察受限空间中的接触选择、自由基座负载移位与全身平衡。A free-base Unitree G1 with Dex3 hands, simulated with Genesis GPU contact physics, must move a compact refrigerator wedged in a corner entirely into a floor goal zone. Walls and a fixed cabinet block its back and sides so it cannot be turned in place; it can only be pulled out by its front handle, dragged along the floor, then released to settle. The task tests contact selection in confined space, free-base load relocation and whole-body balance.

条件Conditions
名义配置。冰箱 8 kg、44 × 44 × 80 cm，正面一根横把手；背面与一侧贴墙、另一侧为固定柜子，间隙 35 mm；地面摩擦 0.16–0.22；目标区在开口前方地面。超时 240 s；物理 200 Hz、控制 50 Hz；参考解读取特权仿真状态。Nominal configuration. The refrigerator is 8 kg, 44 × 44 × 80 cm, with one transverse front handle; its back and one side face walls and the other side a fixed cabinet, with 35 mm gaps; floor friction 0.16–0.22; the goal zone is on the floor in front of the opening. 240 s timeout; physics at 200 Hz, control at 50 Hz; the reference reads privileged simulator state.
任务设计Task design
冰箱整体（含把手）须进入目标区、保持直立并由地面支撑，松手后与机器人无接触并静置两秒；不得倾倒或推动墙体和柜子。原地大角度转动需要约 91 mm 侧隙，现有只有 35 mm，只能先拉出。参考专家：侧身建立双脚支撑 → 右手抓正面横把手 → 小幅拉出 → 带接触后退并修正横向位置 → 松手静置。The whole refrigerator, handle included, must end inside the goal zone, upright and floor-supported, out of contact with the robot and still for two seconds after release, without tipping it or moving the walls or cabinet. Turning it in place would need about 91 mm of side clearance against the 35 mm available, so it must be pulled out first. Reference expert: sideways double-support stance → right hand grips the front handle → short pull-out → walk backward in contact while correcting lateral position → release and settle.
