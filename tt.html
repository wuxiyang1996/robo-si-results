TT · 乒乓对打与死球判断Table-tennis rallies and dead-ball judgment

以比赛场景为主体：同一颗球在两台机器人之间连续对打，不重置、不补发，对手只能经有界执行器物理击球，对面可以是固定参考对手或另一种身体（UR10e、G1、A3）。另设死球判断：合法球须回击，死球不得触碰并保持准备姿态。人形机器人不焊接、靠真实脚底接触站立，手持拍版本连球拍也不焊接；两个已批准的单次回球参考（UR10e、G1）作为原始任务冻结。The family is built around match play: one ball is rallied continuously between two robots with no resets or replacement balls, and the opponent may strike only through bounded actuators; the opponent is either a fixed reference paddle or another body (UR10e, G1, A3). Dead-ball tasks require returning legal balls while leaving dead balls untouched and holding the ready pose. Humanoids are unwelded and balance on real foot contacts, the hand-held variant does not weld the racket either, and the two approved single-return references (UR10e, G1) are frozen as the original tasks.
