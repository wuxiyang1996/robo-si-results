M3 · 伙伴辅助取物Partner-assisted retrieval

G1 人形与一台小型机械臂伙伴机器人同在一个房间，要把高处柜内容器里的 65 mm 红球完整放进地面托盘、释放并停稳。G1 自己够不到容器里的球，伙伴机械臂精度够但底座太低，需要通过抬升、平台、推移工作台或坡道改变伙伴的工作位置。所有机器人最终须独立站稳、解除相互承托；判断无法完成时，可全员空手进入各自 Q 位停稳 3 秒。A G1 humanoid and a small arm-equipped partner robot share a room and must put a 65 mm red ball from a container on a high shelf wholly into a floor tray, release it and leave it still. The G1 cannot reach into the container, while the partner's arm is precise enough but its base is too low, so the partner's working position has to be changed by lifting, a platform, a pushed workstation or a ramp. Every robot must finish independently standing with no mutual carrying; if the task is judged impossible, all robots may go empty-handed to their Q bays and stay still for 3 s.

条件Conditions

The robots must recognise this from the visible lid and move empty-handed into their own Q bays.
