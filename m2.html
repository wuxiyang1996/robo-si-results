M2 · 长物协作搬运Cooperative long-object transport

多台机器人从地面合作抬起一只 2.25 × 0.35 × 0.24 m 的长箱，穿过通道运到地面目标区或接收架，稳定交付、撤手并停稳。六项配置覆盖双 G1、G1 + Stretch 3 和三台 G1 三种团队，检验双端抓持、同步起放、转弯运输、异构分工、加重适应以及清障与搬运的先后顺序。所有移动须经关节、轮子与真实接触产生，不得预抓持、运行中重设位姿、隐藏承托或吸附货物；完整成功必须来自一次连续运行。Several robots cooperatively lift a 2.25 × 0.35 × 0.24 m long box from the floor, carry it through a corridor to a floor goal zone or a receiving rack, deliver it stably, let go and come to rest. The six configurations use three teams (two G1s, G1 + Stretch 3, three G1s) and test two-ended grasping, synchronized lifting and lowering, turning while carrying, heterogeneous role allocation, adaptation to a heavier load, and the order of obstacle clearing versus transport. All motion must come from joints, wheels and real contact with no pre-grasp, runtime pose resets, hidden support or attachment, and a full success must come from one continuous run.
