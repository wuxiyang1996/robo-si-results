H1 · 支撑辅助取物Support-assisted retrieval

机器人要利用环境中的凳子、箱子、坡道、平台或工具改变自己的工作空间，从高处或深处取出目标箱，返回地面后把它完整送入交付区 D、释放并保持稳定。22 项任务覆盖 G1＋Dex3、换装五指手的 G1、A3、轮式 R1 和 Stretch 3，包括只能推的支撑、多候选选择、钩子取物、同色替身和窄门洞。另有三项不可完成对照（H1S-06、H1S-09、H1S-11.C），正确行为是不取物、空手进入 Q 区停稳。The robot must use stools, boxes, ramps, platforms or tools in the scene to change its own workspace, take a target box from a high or deep place, come back down and deliver it wholly into zone D, released and stable. The 22 tasks cover G1 + Dex3, G1 with a five-finger hand, A3, the wheeled R1 and Stretch 3, including push-only supports, choosing among candidates, hook retrieval, same-colour decoys and a narrow doorway. Three infeasible controls (H1S-06, H1S-09, H1S-11.C) require the robot not to retrieve but to exit empty-handed into zone Q and stand still.
