Case-specific hierarchical witness: two-G1 hidden-map box clearing
2026-10-26

Acceptance
The requirement is that an expert plan can be realized in a concrete declared
configuration. We check conversions between representations, not arbitrary
pairwise skill composition or solvability from every random state:
  High level -> low level: each executed operation has an action/constraint expansion.
  Low level -> trajectory: each expansion maps to generated motor targets and
    measured physical states in the original continuous execution.
  High level -> trajectory: measured postconditions realize the intended subgoal.
  End to end: the same complete execution reaches both robots' own goals and
    passes the declared sampled physical checks.
Universal rolling planning, new-layout robustness and arbitrary skill entry
states are separate work. They are not prerequisites for this case-level witness.
Random draws retain full-success / screened-feasible / unknown / rejected labels;
this annotation does not promote any other instance to full-success evidence.

Selected expert and provenance
Reuse native Gamma job 8011370, the observation-matched expert on the four-crate
development layout. The agents used own RGB, proprioception and explicit peer
messages; evaluator global poses were not policy inputs. The high-level plan
comes from recorded action_start/action_completed events. Its low-level actions
come from the frozen HiddenAgent state machine and learned leg controller.
The trajectory is the original joint-target and measured-state trace, with
complete public-input replay as separate generation-provenance evidence.
The hierarchy annotation is retrospective; no new physical rollout was run.

There are 24 high-level operations (19 walks and 5 pushes), 76 low-level segments,
13,959 control steps and 27,918 per-robot joint-target vectors over 279.18 s.
54 segments expand the operations; 22 retain initial stabilization, exploration,
communication waits, operation handoffs and final goal standing. Both robots'
commands cover the whole episode without gaps, duplicate intervals or inserted
standalone probes. Their concurrent time intervals are checked separately.

Time semantics and integrity
Command i drives [i*0.02,(i+1)*0.02]; trace row i is the resulting physical state.
An action includes its completion tick. A phase change inside a control branch
affects the next tick, while action_start precedes the current tick's branch.
The adapter accounts for that distinction instead of blindly copying debug
labels. Preparing, releasing and standing are part of the recorded execution.
The checker verifies original artifact hashes, all 46 frozen source hashes,
the supported controller version, continuous per-robot coverage, complete phase
expansions and each operation's measured postcondition. A different controller
requires a reviewed phase adapter; a mismatched version fails closed.

Physical scope and grader
Reuse the original independent review: 3 s simultaneous own-goal dwell (2 s
required), actuator limits, posture, crate support/tilt and sampled contacts.
Maximum crate tilt is 0.638041 degrees; floor-bottom deviation is 1.537887 mm;
inter-robot contact is zero; non-hand crate contact above 10 N totals 0.78 s.
The phase mapping does not add every-physics-substep or complete no-grasp
certification. Formal task_success remains null and formal admission false.
The case witness passes within the declared sampled scope. Neither reproducing
this expert path nor executing this exact skill sequence is a task scoring
requirement. A different valid solution is graded by the same task conditions.
