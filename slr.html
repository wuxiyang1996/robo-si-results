SLR · 狭缝工具取物Narrow-slot tool retrieval

自由基座 Unitree G1（Dex3 手）要从带顶盖的 45 mm 水平窄缝深处取出小盒子。G1 腕部比窄缝宽，正面直接伸手够不到，须抓起台面上的自由钩杆，控制工具位姿越过目标后分段回拉，再把盒子完整放入台面目标区并脱离。考察工具获取、工具位姿控制以及可达性与碰撞推理。A free-base Unitree G1 with Dex3 hands must retrieve a small box from deep inside a roofed 45 mm horizontal slot. The G1 wrist is wider than the slot, so a straight frontal reach cannot get there; the robot must pick up a free hook tool from the table, steer it past the target, pull back in stages, then leave the box fully inside a tabletop goal zone and disengage. The task tests tool acquisition, tool pose control, and reachability and collision reasoning.

条件Conditions
名义配置。35 mm、60 g 黄色小盒子位于 0.60 m 高台面上的窄缝深处；窄缝宽 45 mm、净高约 160 mm、长 0.50 m，两侧与顶盖固定；台面上放有一根 0.46 m 自由钩杆；摩擦 0.28–0.40。超时 240 s；物理 200 Hz、控制 50 Hz；参考解读取特权仿真状态。Nominal configuration. A 35 mm, 60 g yellow box sits deep inside a slot on a 0.60 m work surface; the slot is 45 mm wide, about 160 mm high and 0.50 m long, with fixed sides and roof; a free 0.46 m hook tool lies on the table; friction 0.28–0.40. 240 s timeout; physics at 200 Hz, control at 50 Hz; the reference reads privileged simulator state.
任务设计Task design
盒子须完整放入台面目标区，由台面支撑、与机器人和工具均无接触并静置两秒；不得掉落，不得移动固定结构。G1 腕部内切球直径约 52.37 mm，大于 45 mm 缝宽，正面直接伸手不可达（只覆盖正面直伸，不是全局证明）。参考专家：抓起钩杆并拇指对握 → 在入口外校正位姿 → 越过目标后下落勾住 → 最多三段短拉，段间放开由台面支撑的目标并后退 0.12 m 换位 → 脱离并静置。The box must end fully inside the tabletop goal zone, supported by the table, out of contact with robot and tool and still for two seconds, without dropping it or moving the fixed structure. The G1 wrist's inscribed sphere (about 52.37 mm) exceeds the 45 mm slot, so a straight frontal reach is excluded (this covers only frontal reaching, not a global proof). Reference expert: grasp the hook with thumb opposition → correct its pose outside the entrance → pass beyond the target and drop the hook behind it → at most three short pulls, releasing the table-supported box and stepping back 0.12 m between pulls → disengage and settle.
