F1 · 柔顺工具使用与装配Compliant Tool Use and Assembly

F1 考察借助工具与接触界面的柔顺性完成无损装配：判断何时施力、何时改变方法、何时停止。三个子任务分别是紧配合零件压入与敲合、经保护垫传力的保护性装配，以及两台机器人协作的框架装配。配合阻力由被动弹簧夹爪与摩擦产生，没有插入马达、抓取焊接或成功即锁定的机关；每项都有橡胶接触锤与刚体金属锤两个版本。F1 tests damage-free assembly that relies on compliance in tools and contact interfaces: deciding when to apply force, when to change method and when to stop. Its three subtasks are press-fit insertion and mallet seating, protected assembly through an intermediate pad, and cooperative frame assembly by two robots. Fit resistance comes from passive spring-loaded jaws and friction, with no insertion motor, grasp weld or success-triggered latch; every task has a rubber-contact and a rigid metal hammer version.
