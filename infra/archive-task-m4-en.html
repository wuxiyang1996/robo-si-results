Boundary with M2

In M2, robots support the cargo through hand contact: cooperative lifting, grasp retention, load sharing and synchronized lowering are central. In M4, the ground supports the cargo through a platform or similar support while robots provide traction or steering: tension, slip and towing geometry are central. Two G1s lifting a box or carrying frame belong to M2; two G1s towing a ground-supported platform belong to M4.

Counting rules and development order

  • Straight pulls and breakaway tests are capability probes. A single Go2 is a control for M4-01/03, not a seventh main variant.
  • Keep separate base/hard configurations and results within M4-01. Tool/team selection, unequal capabilities and recovery are configuration or evaluation conditions.
  • First: M4-01 regression, M4-02 single G1 and M4-03 three Go2s. M4-04/05 follow at P1; M4-06 is P2.
  • Start preloaded and preconnected. Validate actual hand grasp, tool operation, autonomous attachment and detachment separately; a fixed attachment does not establish grasping, and state edits do not establish physical reconnection.

A failed solo attempt does not prove cooperation necessary, and a failed three-robot formation does not rule out other formations. If the third robot may idle without interference, evaluate whether calling it is worthwhile. Listed roles are author-side candidate designs, not optimal-role hints for the evaluation agent.

Retain the original configuration's 400 N rope rating, 50 mm slip limit and final dwell criterion. Validate G1 interfaces and biped contact monitoring separately; do not substitute the autonomous connector's 150 N fixture evidence for the original towing reference.
