01 · Task and scope

Fit the blue sleeve and then the red cap onto the rectangular locator of a hinged platform. Before inserting the sleeve, keep the platform horizontal and slow, supported by released objects resting on the table for 0.5 seconds. Supports may consist of one or more objects.

Seat the sleeve on the platform, release it and hold it stable for 0.5 seconds before inserting the cap. Seat the cap on the sleeve while maintaining the platform support chain. Finally, keep every object slow and free from all robot contact for 2 seconds.

Scoring uses physical states and load paths without requiring a particular support identity, placement point or motion route. The robot body cannot replace released support. Both parts cannot be inserted simultaneously. A 180° rotation fits the rectangular hole; 90° does not.

Wrong-order assembly, losing established support qualification continuously for 0.1 seconds, dropping an object or exceeding 120 seconds fails. Re-contact or instability resets the final hold timer. Eight inputs cover five fixed seeds, two range boundaries and a nominal scene. No distant heavy block or fixed distractor appears in this layer. Grading randomizes per episode when --randomized is selected.

Fixed-pelvis Unitree G1 with Dex3 hands, 43 absolute joint targets, 50 Hz control and 200 Hz physics, fixed-gain PD and hardware torque limits. Arm limits vary between 85% and 100% of nominal and initial arm poses may vary. Evaluation uses robot sensors; the development reference uses privileged state.

03 · Scoring criteria

Only full physical success scores 1; every other result scores 0. Stage progress is diagnostic. Task scoring, independent terminal-state checks and monitors must agree.

  1. A released, grounded physical load path supports the horizontal platform for 0.5 s before sleeve insertion.
  2. The sleeve must fit the post and sit on the platform, released and stable for 0.5 s before cap insertion.
  3. The cap must fit the same post and sit on the sleeve while support persists.
  4. All objects must be released and slow, with the complete structure stable for 2 s.

Objects must move through actual contact. Teleportation, binding, suction, fabricated contact or score modification is forbidden. Check penetration, abnormal energy, jitter and continued robot support. The final pipeline includes positives, zero-score no-op, privileged teleportation counterexamples, 38 container escape probes and integrity checks.

Each effective upward support force must exceed 0.05 N. Every load-bearing branch must be grounded and released; held branches, floating cycles, unknown carriers and missing measurements fail.
