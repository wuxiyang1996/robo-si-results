Declared scope

Fixed-pelvis G1 with two Dex3 hands; only the declared seed 0 profile is verified. Three complete runs start from independent resets, followed by a separate action replay. The default smaller development scene and other randomized profiles are outside this evidence.

Two lower rails support three upper pieces and a cap; support materials are provided nearby. The declared long wedge is 320 × 28 × 55 mm with an 80 mm entrance ramp and a 51 mm tip. Its flat body matches the rail height. The task goal does not prescribe the operation order.

Interface and observations

43 joint-angle targets, 50 Hz control and 200 Hz physics evaluation, with model torque caps and PD control. The reference reads privileged object poses, velocities and contacts. It uses no object-pose action, attachment or external-force action.

Reference plan

Slide and align the wedge on the table. The right hand temporarily steadies the cap while the left thumb pushes the wedge from its rear. Withdraw both hands, then use two low end grasps to extract the target. Regrasp centrally, lift, rotate, deliver and release. Load transfer occurs through physical contact.

Scoring

The target must clear horizontally before lifting and delivery. Protected upper-piece displacement over 18 mm, orientation error over 15 degrees, drops or timeout fail the task. Success requires the delivered target to contact the table and both target and upper structure to remain quiet and released for 3 seconds. Dismantling the upper structure is forbidden.

Execution and replay evidence

All 3 declared complete runs passed, with a separate full action replay. The representative run lasts 304.550 seconds. There are also 9 passing static mechanical diagnostics; these contain no robot action and are not expert runs. Development failures are retained separately.
