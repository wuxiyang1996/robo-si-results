A G1 with two Dex3 hands uses 43 joint targets to manipulate a 200 mm diameter, 12 mm thick, 120 g disk. The table is 0.86 m high, with its near edge at x = 0.35 m. The orange face starts up; the blue face must finish up.

Scoring and success criteria

Native score = float(success); stage progress is diagnostic. Edge positioning, bimanual clamp, clearance, inversion and placement/release must occur in order; a final pose alone cannot pass. Edge dwell: 0.25 s; clamp and inversion: 0.30 s each; final stability: 2 s. Goal tolerance: 45 mm; flatness: 8 degrees; contact-loss grace: 0.10 s; timeout: 600 s. Force and speed limits are conservative task settings, not calibrated material-damage thresholds.
