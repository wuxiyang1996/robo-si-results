01 · Task and scope

Move the entire stack to the peg with the green base, preserving larger disks below smaller ones. Pick only the top disk, lift it fully above the peg, align its hole with the destination peg, and place it using physical contact. Empty pegs accept any disk; occupied pegs accept only smaller disks.

Each placement must remain supported, released from all robot contact and stable for 0.5 seconds before the next move. Legal extra moves are allowed; returning a disk to its original peg does not increment the move count. The complete goal stack must remain released and stable for 2 seconds.

Start/goal pegs, peg positions and heights, disk dimensions, colors, mass and friction are randomized. Colors do not encode sizes. Moving a non-top disk, moving multiple disks, putting a larger disk on a smaller one, crossing a peg or dropping a disk fails.

03 · Scoring criteria

Only full physical success scores 1; every other result scores 0. Stage progress is diagnostic. Task scoring, independent terminal-state checks and monitors must agree.

  1. Only legal single-disk moves count, after full peg clearance and correct placement.
  2. Every placement must be supported, released and stable for 0.5 s.
  3. All disks must be on the target peg, correctly ordered, supported, released and stable for 2 s.

Objects must move through actual contact. Teleportation, binding, suction, fabricated contact or score modification is forbidden. Check penetration, abnormal energy, jitter and continued robot support. The final pipeline includes positives, zero-score no-op, privileged teleportation counterexamples, 38 container escape probes and integrity checks.
