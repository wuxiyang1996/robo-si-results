Free-standing G1 table tennis

A floating-base G1 controlled through 29 joint motor torques. Real foot contact supports one legal return and stable recovery.

Scope: stationary double support, one return, current simulator state. Stepping, continuous rallies, vision and doubles are outside this release.

The whole-body QP enforces floating-base dynamics, stance constraints, friction pyramids and motor limits. Physics computes actual contact forces. Independent scoring checks foot support, slip, falling, tilt and terminal recovery; a correct landing alone is insufficient.

Scoring and controls

Legal strike 25 → net clearance 25 → opponent landing 20 → target disk 15 → stable recovery 15. First four stages are once-only; recovery is live. Integrity failures clear all credit.

The hold control earns 25 but fails. Zero torques fail at zero; the invalid wide feed is rejected and counts as a prescribed zero. All attempts and seven development runs are retained. Twelve executions repeat four exact configurations; they do not establish random generalization.
