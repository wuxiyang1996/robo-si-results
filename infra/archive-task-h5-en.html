01 · Task and scope

This release scope contains layer 1 only. Layers 2 and 3, alternate-body utility and R9 are deferred. Physics guards, first-layer random coverage, standalone subtasks, human acceptance and independent reproduction remain required.

Task goal (agent-facing summary)

Move the heavy crate into the green goal region, upright and at rest, supported by the floor or floor-standing objects for at least two seconds. Objects in the room may be used. Transport credit requires the crate to ride on other objects; before delivery, it must rest on its supports without robot contact. Execute through the original joint-command interface under the original motor, collision and stability limits.

This is a goal summary. The canonical repository instruction still says 65 kg, while this evidence uses the explicit 100 kg development candidate. The formal entry point and instruction must be aligned before release. Current canonical instruction

Scene and randomisation (designer/reviewer only)

G1 + Dex3, 43 joints; Genesis 1.4.3, 200 Hz physics and 50 Hz control with hardware torque limits. Cargo: 100 kg, 0.60 × 0.54 × 0.48 m. Goal: 1.6 × 1.6 m; dwell: 2 s; non-foot collision limit: 800 N.
