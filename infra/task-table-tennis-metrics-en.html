Table-tennis extensions: G1 and robot arms

One task family for interception, continuous rallies and relocation during rallies. Ball metrics are shared; stepping, fixed-arm and rail constraints are validated separately.

Metrics and denominators

Full success rateComplete physical task successes / all prescribed episodes
Legal-return coverageLegal prefix count / prescribed return budget; uncompleted shots stay in the denominator
Rally survivalFraction reaching 1/3/5/10/20 returns, grouped by prescribed horizon
Landing precisionTarget hit rate, target coverage, median/p90 legal landing error and sample count
Relocation completionOn-time assigned relocations / all prescribed relocation slots; fixed arm is N/A
Physical validityFalls, collisions, limits, loaded-foot slip, rail travel and observed-trace coverage
Recovery and efficiencyTerminal recovery; energy per legal return and replanning latency as secondary diagnostics

Stage scores: 100 points

I: positioning 10 + legal strike 25 + net 20 + opponent first landing 20 + target 15 + recovery 10.

R: 80 C/N + 10 H/N + 10 R_end

MR: 60 C/N + 20 P/M + 10 H/N + 10 R_end

C is the initial valid return prefix, H its target hits, and P completed assigned relocations. Draft targets are N=10 and M=4. R_end is gated terminal recovery after all required continuity and movement conditions.

R with C=6 and H=4 followed by a miss scores 52 and fails. MR with C=10, H=8 and P=3/4 scores 83 and fails. Report the three task scores separately.

Validity rules

  • Stepping permits single-foot support. Lift, movement and load-bearing landing require physical evidence; dragging or stepping after impact earns no credit.
  • A fixed arm has no step metric. A rail arm must move an inertial carriage through bounded physical actuation.
  • A rally alternates physical returns of the same ball. Independent machine feeds are a separate endurance task.
  • First failure ends the valid prefix. No best-subsequence selection, ball replacement or shrinking N/M. Opponent faults stay in the cohort and are attributed separately.
  • Full success is separate from score. Falling revokes recovery; missing, reset or forged evidence is VOID and scores zero.
Existing UR10e and stationary G1 single-return references each have 12/12 successes and 4/4 replays. They do not validate these extensions. Movement, support, timing and recovery thresholds are proposed pending calibration; new expert trajectories, graders and physical acceptance remain unimplemented.
