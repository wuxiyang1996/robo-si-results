Scope and remaining acceptance

  • Fixed hooks-v0.1 scene; G1 raises and carries X5, with box support during shelf picking. Not continuously airborne picking.
  • Original 65 mm ball retained. Opening is 40.5 mm per finger within the public 44 mm stroke, avoiding recontact from the former 64 mm total gap.
  • First 222 s replay recorded LowCmd with tracking aborts; feedback return, lowering and delivery follow. Privileged state/contact information is used; no ordinary RPC or general-policy success claim.
  • Both same-author, same-parameter runs are array-identical; they are not independent seeds or second-person reproduction.
  • Route search fell back to a fixed direct path. General planning is unverified. Original 420 s limit, mass, friction, scoring and benchmark monitors are unchanged.
  • Human video/stage sign-off, second-person reproduction, random and standalone coverage, shortcut evidence and formal admission remain pending; admitted=false.
Engineering changes to motion and contact guards

Some old millimeter tracking/foot-anchor guards remain as warnings, while stopping uses support, floor transfer and geometry conditions. Whitelisted upward support contacts use an exploratory 5 mm cap; observed peak is 3.705 mm. Other contact guards remain. This is an engineering choice, not a user-specified or hardware-certified tolerance, and requires physical review. Mass, friction, hardware and formal scoring thresholds are unchanged.
