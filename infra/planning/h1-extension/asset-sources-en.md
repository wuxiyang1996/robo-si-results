## Actual Gamma probes

Slurm array `8035305` ran with maximum concurrency one GPU, at most 2 simulated seconds and 20 wall minutes per model. Physics used `.005 s` steps, a free root, a ground plane and self-collision enabled. No balancing, locomotion or manipulation controller was applied; only passive dynamics from default joints were observed.

| Wall seconds | 64.82 | 39.87 |
| Final root linear speed, m/s | 0.597261 | 0.000139 |

Both assets loaded and produced finite, continuous measured states. Source and referenced mesh hashes remained unchanged. Despite `enable_self_collision=True`, Genesis filtered neutral-pose pairs by default: **this is not full collision certification**. A3 did not settle under a stabilizing controller, and Stretch4's low endpoint speed does not establish manipulation, ramp or load stability. Neither result establishes task infeasibility or asset unsuitability. Both retain `stable_pose_claimed=false`, `task_success=null` and `counts_as_H1_task_run=false`.

The evidence directory contains the frozen probe code, predeclared budget, environment, raw Slurm logs, all 401 state rows per model, collision-pair lists and byte-hash manifests. Full local source caches remain under `/fs/gamma-projects/vlm-robot/robo-si-local-tests/h1-implementation-20261027/asset-cache/`; original license files are retained.
