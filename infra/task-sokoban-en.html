Scope and scene

Four-crate development map; own RGB, proprioception and explicit messages; one complete experimental witness

Gamma 8011370: 279.18 s, 24 high-level operations and 76 low-level segments; both robots dwell at their own goals for 3 s. Independent sampled review and complete public-input replay pass; task_success=null.

Two G1 robots clear routes by pushing boxes and reach their own color-coded goals. The boxes have no delivery goals. Grid spacing is 0.9 m; four crates are 0.65 × 0.65 × 0.55 m, 4 kg each, with friction 0.4. Evidence is one continuous development-map witness, Gamma 8011370, not unseen-map generalization.

Sandbox README · Agent SDK

Each robot sees only its own RGB, joint/inertial observations, target identity and explicit received messages: no global map, true crate poses or hidden peer state. Messages are limited to 256 UTF-8 bytes and 16 pending per recipient. Each submits 43 absolute joint targets; the shared world advances only after both submit. Control is 50 Hz; native dt=0.005 s and control_decimation=4. Development perception assumes calibrated inertial increments, flat ground, known grid spacing and color-coded materials.

Scoring and success criteria

Arrival diagnostics require each robot inside its own 0.85 × 0.85 m goal region, standing and slow, with simultaneous dwell. Native requirement is 2 s; the witness records 3 s. Limits: linear speed 0.05 m/s, angular speed 0.1 rad/s, tilt 10 degrees and root height at least 0.45 m. Arrival does not certify legal push-only behavior: task_success=null and the new sandbox withholds task credit (0). Cell-center errors and controller guards are not extra scoring rules.
