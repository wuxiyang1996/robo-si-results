01 · Task design

Complementary bodies, one continuous task

A 4.5 cm yellow cube sits in a plastic crate on a 0.90 m shelf; the crate rim reaches 1.10 m. The destination is a tray deep in a low cabinet: 0.62 m internal height and a tray center about 0.80 m behind the opening. Cabinet support must begin before both robots release the cube, which must remain stable.

Unitree G1 + Dex3

Handles the elevated crate and lowers the handover height.

ARX X5 + mobile base

Retrieves from the stand, enters the cabinet and places with support. The mobile base is custom; the 40 N finger conversion is a modeling assumption.

Ordinary agents use robot sensors and public joint interfaces only. The budget is 300 s and 40,000 observations, with 3 resets in development mode. Other compliant routes are allowed; the reference action sequence is not mandatory.

Images come from the sealed simulation: the Unitree / MuJoCo Menagerie G1 model, Poly Haven shelf and plastic crate assets, plus task-specific cabinet, stand and base geometry. Source attribution and licenses are retained in the full source packet.

03 · Scoring

Stage credit and final success are checked separately

m1-scoring/2-supported-release · Stage peaks contribute to the score; final success requires delivered to remain true at termination and validity checks to pass.

StageWeightPhysical predicate summary
container_handled10%Hand/finger contact of at least 3 N for 0.3 s, with the crate or cube displaced by at least 3 cm.
off_shelf20%Cube at least 5 cm beyond the shelf front, not falling, with robots upright for 1 s. Distance gives partial credit.
mobile_grasp20%Both fingers exert at least 1 N for 0.2 s for partial credit; sole mobile-gripper support without G1 contact for 1 s for full credit.
in_cabinet20%Depth inside the cabinet earns partial credit; reaching above the tray earns full credit.
delivered30%The true cube bounds fit in the tray. Cabinet support precedes final robot release. Robot force < 0.3 N, support > half its weight, speed ≤ 0.03 m/s and angular speed ≤ 0.5 rad/s, with a 2 s dwell that remains valid at the end.

G1 falls, base tipping, excessive drop speed and monitor violations affect success or validity. All 14 targeted synthetic scorer cases pass; these are scorer tests, not additional physical successes.
