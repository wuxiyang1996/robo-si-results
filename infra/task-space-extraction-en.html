Measured paths, forces and scores

Independent scoring

Success is 100 × valid complete delivery; physical progress is separate. The complete object must be contained, supported, released from robot and tool, and quiet for two seconds. Falls, target drop or excessive tilt, speed limits, structure contact above 150 N, illegal state mutation or missing measurements prevent success. Expert-route imitation is not a scoring condition.

Randomization and evidence scope

All 22 prescribed draws are retained: five low-level seeds, five high-level seeds and one nominal case per task. Low-level draws vary lateral position, depth and friction; high-level draws change appliance delivery direction or retrieval exit/tool strategy. Only two exact nominal instances are witnessed_full; 20 remain unknown. Screening earns no execution score. Turning and far-exit pushing have no validated executors.

The nominal front-entry hand-access check uses G1 collision meshes: a 52.37 mm wrist insphere exceeds the 45 mm slot. This is a front-access bound, not a global impossibility proof.
